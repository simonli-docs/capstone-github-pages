# Welcome!

Welcome to this document site: Capstone Design Guide.

The purpose of this Guide is to support engineering students to work on their capstone design projects. Our support primarily aims to answer one question: **What should students do in a capstone design project?**

Instead of doing our design work intuitively, this Guide will outline and explain how we can organize our design work through five types of [**cognitive tasks**](chapter_1_introduction/#14-cognitive-design-tasks).

- Background research
- Problem framing
- Idea generation
- Decision making
- Scientific analysis

Also, we can organize our capstone design process in three [**design stages**](chapter_1_introduction/#13-capstone-design-process): (1) conceptual design, (2) design development, and (3) design verification. Each design stage has its own purpose and typical design activities.
